## Context

Human: 'Yes, consider best practices for data-residency'; DPDP Act proposed from that wording and the India-only guest base, to be confirmed

## Decision

Treat guest details as personal data under India's DPDP Act: collect with consent, minimise what is stored, support access and erasure requests, and notify on breach. Role-based access for staff.
